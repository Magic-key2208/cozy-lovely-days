Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

The short version

  • The game has no chat and no way to talk to strangers.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your own Google account so you can get it back on a new phone. We do not have servers.
  • You don't need to create an account: you can sign in with Google Play Games or play without signing in.
  • The game can show optional video ads from Unity Ads. For children and for players whose age we don't know, ads are not personalized and the advertising ID is not used.

1. Who we are

Your Cozy Lovely Days (“the game”) is made by Viktoriia Kinziabulatova (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions about this policy, write to v.kinzyabulatova22@gmail.com.

2. What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following information on your device:

  • game progress: coins, crystals, inventory, recipes, cooking timers and level;
  • your character's appearance and the name you choose for it;
  • settings such as language and sound;
  • your answer to the year-of-birth question, if you give one (see section 4).

This information is kept on your device. The game does not send it to us — we do not have servers, cannot see it and cannot restore it ourselves. The only copies outside your device are the ones described in section 5: a cloud copy of your progress in Google Play Games and Android's own backup, both in your own Google account.

3. Advertising

The game can show a video ad when you choose to watch one — for example, to make cooking faster. Watching ads is never required to play.

Ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). When an ad is requested or shown, Unity receives information from your device. This may include your IP address, device model and operating system version, language, approximate location based on the IP address, information about the ad and whether it was watched, and — only where section 4 allows it — your device's advertising ID. Unity uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, prevent fraud and measure results.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

4. Children and the age question

The game is made for players of all ages, including children. Before the first ad, the game asks for a year of birth. It does not suggest which answer to give, and the answer is used for one purpose only: to choose which kind of ads may be shown.

  • If the player is under 13, or has not answered, ads are child-directed: no personalized ads, no advertising ID, and only ads suitable for all ages.
  • If the player is under 16, or has not answered, ads are not personalized.
  • Personalized ads may be shown only to players aged 16 or over and, where the law requires it, only with their consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please write to v.kinzyabulatova22@gmail.com.

5. Google Play, Google Play Games and backups

The game is distributed through Google Play. Downloads, payments and reviews are handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cloud save (Google Play Games). When the game starts, it signs in to Google Play Games automatically if your device has a Play Games profile. If not, you can tap “Sign in with Google Play Games” on the first screen (and create a profile there) or play without signing in. After you sign in, a copy of your game progress — everything in section 2 except your year of birth — is saved with Google Play Games in your own Google account, so you can get it back after reinstalling the game or on a new phone. If the cloud and your phone have different progress, the game asks you which one to keep. Google stores this copy under its privacy policy; we have no servers and cannot see it. Children under 13 can have a Play Games profile only with a parent's approval (Family Link); without a profile, the game saves progress only on the device.

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may also copy the game's data to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game. You can turn this off in your Android settings.

6. How long information is kept and how to delete it

Everything listed in section 2 is kept until you delete it. “Delete progress” on the profile screen in the game deletes it from the device and also deletes the cloud copy in Google Play Games. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on the device. You can also delete the game's data saved in Google Play Games in the Google Play Games settings, and Android backups in your Android or Google account settings.

Information received by Unity is kept according to Unity's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually under Privacy → Ads).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because we do not hold information about you, most of this concerns the data on your own device, which you control directly (section 6). For information collected by Unity, you can use Unity's privacy tools or contact Unity. We are always happy to help: v.kinzyabulatova22@gmail.com.

8. Security

The game does not ask for an email address or a password. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect other information — for example, if online features are added — we will update this policy before that happens and change the date at the top of this page.
